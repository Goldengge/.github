### Core Capabilities & Features

* **3D Volumetric Rendering:** Generates true 3D volumetric models (`VolRender`) from gridded data, allowing users to adjust opacity, color gradients, and lighting to inspect internal structures such as underground contamination plumes, ore deposits, or atmospheric data.
* **Isosurfaces & Contours:** Creates 3D surfaces of constant value (isosurfaces) and 3D contour lines to clearly define boundaries within volumetric data—such as threshold concentrations of pollutants or specific temperature zones.
* **Borehole & Well Modeling:** Displays drillhole and well log data (`WellRender`) in 3D space, showing trajectories, downhole logs, lithology intervals, and deviation surveys.
* **Cross-Sections & Slices:** Enables users to slice through 3D models at any angle using orthogonal (`OrthoImage`) or oblique (`ObliqueImage`) planes and clip planes to expose interior cross-sections.
* **Point Clouds, Vectors & Streamlines:** Supports LiDAR point clouds, 3D scatter plots, vector plots (showing magnitude and direction), and streamlines for modeling fluid or airflow paths.
* **3D Gridding & Math Module:** Converts scattered XYZC (X, Y, Z coordinates plus a component value) data into regular 3D lattices using interpolation methods like Inverse Distance, Kriging, and Data Metrics, and allows custom mathematical operations across grids.

### Common Use Cases

* **Environmental Science:** Modeling groundwater contamination plumes and soil remediation zones.
* **Geology & Mining:** Visualizing mineral deposits, stratigraphic layers, seismic surveys, and borehole logs.
* **Geophysics & Engineering:** Inspecting subsurface radar (GPR), electrical resistivity tomography (ERT), and geotechnical site investigations.
* **Meteorology & Oceanography:** Rendering atmospheric pressure systems, wind vectors, ocean temperatures, and salinity profiles.
